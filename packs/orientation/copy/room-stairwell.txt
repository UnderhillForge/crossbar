Concrete, a yellow rail, and a painted arrow that has been touched up so many times the arrow is mostly touch-up. The air smells like a school gym that apologized.

A paper sign, laminated and then immediately regretted, lists the floor in a handwriting that wanted to be a font:

  kiosk — safety, allegedly
  vending — do not negotiate
  tutorial-arena — the practice bow is cardboard
  superintendent-office — locked, and not in a fun way

The booth's speaker is a circle of holes over the landing. It crackles when it is about to be a person.

Nothing on the wall is the pamphlet. Nothing on the rail is the night-shift note. The stairwell has had its chance to be useful and spent it on the arrow.

You can go back up with bye, which returns you to Terminal Addiction and not, the booth would like to stress, to a real operating system.
