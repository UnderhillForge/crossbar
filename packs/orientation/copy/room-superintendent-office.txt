The door is locked. The lock is a lock. The booth would like this sentence to end there, and yet.

A plaque, brass and slightly ashamed:

  SUPERINTENDENT
  If you are here for the notes, you have already walked past them.
  They are not in the office. The office is where notes go to become
  somebody else's problem. Try the machine that does not work.
  The receipt knows the filename. The filename is not a speech.

Knocking produces a knock. The door does not produce a superintendent. No one is inside being hurt, hiding, or waiting to be clever. The room is locked because the floor has a locked room, and the prize was moved on purpose to a file a receipt can name.

{crawler}, you are still in the hall. The office did not become your room. Going here again will produce this same plaque, because the plaque does not get bored. You might.

Turn around. The stairwell is the honest exit.
